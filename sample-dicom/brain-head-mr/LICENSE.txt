This distribution contains a complete 193-slice axial volume and a mid-sagittal
reference plane from the ICBM152 2009c average T1 template, converted to DICOM
for an educational viewing example.
No interpolation or new anatomy was generated. Original int16 voxel values and
NIfTI intensity scaling are retained. It is not an individual patient scan.
